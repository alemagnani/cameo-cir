\section{Problem, Goal, Deadlines, Submission}
\textbf{Problem.} Composed Image Retrieval: given a \emph{reference product image} + a
\emph{natural-language modification} (``similar but cheaper'', ``same format from brand X''),
return a \emph{ranked list} from a fixed corpus of \textbf{4{,}075 face-cleanser products}
(Vietnamese e-commerce). Zero-shot; test labels withheld until after submission. Metrics: \textbf{NDCG@K, MAP}.

\textbf{Deadlines} (confirmed from \texttt{cameo-task.com}, 2026-06-12): \textbf{team registration due
\underline{Jul 10, 2026 (AOE)}} via the NTCIR-19 system (separate, earlier cliff); \textbf{final runs due
\underline{Jul 20, 2026}}; results released Aug 1; task paper to NTCIR-19 proceedings Oct 2026; workshop
Dec 2026, Tokyo. \emph{Metrics: nDCG@5, nDCG@10 (TREC-style pooled, 3-point human relevance).}
\textbf{Submission:} the \textbf{20 official CIR queries}, format = \texttt{sample\_submission.json}, email to
\texttt{allie.tran@dcu.ie} (subject ``CAMEO Submission - [Team Name]''), \textbf{max 5 runs} named
\texttt{<team>\_CIR\_Run<id>.json}, + workshop paper. \textbf{Schema (verified against the official sample):}
each entry needs \emph{both} \texttt{answer} (ranked list of \emph{image filenames}) \emph{and}
\texttt{answer\_product\_id} (ranked \emph{product ids}); an earlier draft that put ids in \texttt{answer} and
omitted \texttt{answer\_product\_id} was corrected. \textbf{Submission-ready set: \texttt{data/submission/final/}}
--- 5 schema-valid runs, all image filenames map to the corpus (0 unmapped), with \textbf{metadata-fused +
Qwen-72B (\off\ 0.923) promoted to Run1} and bigG+Qwen-72B / rerank variants as Run2--5 for diversity.
Email not yet sent (awaiting go); a later email supersedes before Jul 20.

\section{Data: provided vs.\ created}
\textbf{Provided:} corpus 4{,}075 products (\texttt{brand}, numeric \texttt{price} VND,
\texttt{translated\_name/description}, image; $\sim$1.9\,GB images); 30 official queries (\textbf{20 CIR}
+ 10 QA, no answers); sample-submission template. \textbf{Created by us} ($\sim$\$40 Sonnet; exact GT):
\texttt{queries\_dev} 429 templated (cheaper/pricier\_same\_brand, format/brand/claim\_swap);
rule5/5a/5b LLM free-form (400/646/608, a\&b \textbf{held out}); \texttt{\_full} = \textbf{2{,}083} merged.
\textbf{We use validation only} --- the real test is withheld (no leakage).

Pipeline = \textbf{Stage-1 retrieval} $\to$ \textbf{Stage-2 listwise rerank}. Every variant scored by
\texttt{eval\_by\_rule.py} on \dev\ (per-rule, free) and by the cached judge on \off.

\subsection{Approach \#1 --- LLM/metadata structured retrieval (the new edge)}
The corpus's \texttt{brand}, numeric \texttt{price}, and \texttt{description} are \emph{ignored by visual
CLIP}. \texttt{metadata\_retrieve.py} scores products by brand match $+$ price proximity $+$ format/claim
keyword hits; a \textbf{Haiku extractor} (\texttt{llm\_extract.py}) pulls those fields from free text
(extraction vs gold: brand 100\%, format/claim 100\% present, price 100\% when stated). Metadata-priority
fusion with bigG, \textbf{Stage-1, no rerank}, \dev-429: NDCG@10 \textbf{0.200} (bigG 0.157), R@50
\textbf{0.527} (bigG 0.436). Per-rule big wins: cheaper 0.29 / pricier 0.36 / format 0.23 (the \textbf{price}
signal, never used before). \textbf{On \off: metadata-fused + Qwen-72B = NDCG@10 0.923} --- best official yet.

\subsection{Autoresearch \& negatives}
\textbf{GPU-native autoresearch} (self-driving loop, \textbf{LLM proposer} with the API key kept local via a
producer/consumer split): 21 configs over backbone $\times \alpha \times$ brand-union $\times$ reranker
$\times$ top-K $\to$ \textbf{converged} at bigG+brand-union+Qwen-72B (\dev-429 0.205). Findings: bigG$>$ViT-H,
brand-union helps, top-K=30 best for brand\_swap, $\alpha$ negligible. \textbf{Negatives} (validated cheaply):
rewritten captions \bad, embedding brand fusion \bad, high-$\alpha$ name fusion \bad, RRF ensembling \bad,
symbolic format/claim matching \bad\ (brand+format too tight, pool$\sim$1).

\subsection{Approach \#2 --- MagicLens: attempted, ENV-BLOCKED}
Weights downloaded (base 666MB, large 1.86GB) + adapter written (\texttt{run\_magiclens\_cir.py}). Blocked by
a fragile mid-2024 JAX pin: jax 0.4.30 matches the weights but scenic's TensorFlow needs newer
protobuf/ml\_dtypes; jax 0.10 imports but \emph{segfaults} on the 2024 model; python 3.9 too old for current
flax/optax (4 env attempts). Needs the authors' exact env (their Docker). Deprioritized --- adapter+weights ready.

\end{table}
{\footnotesize $^{\dagger}$Qwen-32B variant. $^{\ddagger}$neutral on full-dev: big win on the structured 21\%,
free-form 79\% has no structure to extract. $^{\S}$rerank \emph{hurts} metadata on exact-match (see below).}

\textbf{What we make of it:}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Recall is the real bottleneck}, not the reranker. On \dev\ the answer was in Phi's top-20
only 8\% of the time; the whole 0.044$\to$0.2 arc is Stage-1 (backbone $+$ metadata), not rerank tuning.
\item \textbf{Local VLM reranking $\ge$ the Haiku API at $\sim$\$0} --- the single most useful operational result.
\item \textbf{Metadata (price/brand/format) is the new lever} and is \emph{orthogonal} to visual reranking:
it gives the best Stage-1 on structured rules and the best \off\ score, but a visual reranker \emph{demotes}
its exact structural matches (0.200$\to$0.173 on exact-\dev). $\Rightarrow$ don't visual-rerank metadata hits.
\item \textbf{\dev\ and \off\ disagree by design} and that's informative: \off\ is recall-saturated/lenient
(everything clusters $\sim$0.9, reranker dominates), \dev\ is strict (separates Stage-1 quality). Trust the
\emph{direction} that agrees on both.
\item \textbf{brand\_swap is the hard rule} ($\sim$0.1) --- brand-name matching in the corpus is the weak link.
\end{itemize}

%==============================================================
\section{What to try next --- and why}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Finalize \& email the 5 runs} (deadline-driven). Promote \textbf{metadata-fused + Qwen-72B}
(\off\ 0.923) as a run; keep Qwen-72B/bigG and top-K=30 variants for diversity against the unknown test.
\item \textbf{Strengthen \#1's brand resolution} --- brand\_swap recall $\sim$0.21 is gated by fuzzy brand-name
matching; better normalization / a brand embedding would lift the single worst rule, and \#1 already works.
\item \textbf{Metadata-\emph{aware} reranker} --- since visual rerank demotes structural matches, keep the
metadata head fixed (or fuse rerank scores with the metadata score) instead of letting the VLM overwrite it.
\item \textbf{MagicLens via the authors' Docker} --- the only clean way to get the 2024 JAX pin; a purpose-built
CIR Stage-1 is the best remaining shot at the visual recall ceiling (ensembling did not break it).
\item \textbf{Generalize \#1 to the natural official queries} --- the extractor already runs on free text; validate
extraction quality on \off\ and confirm the 0.923 holds beyond the 5 structured official queries.
\item \textbf{Fix the MAP$>$1 eval bug} so MAP is trustworthy at K$>$20 before relying on it for submission ranking.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Effort \& cost log (for transparency)}
\textbf{Compute:} $\sim$10 vast.ai GPU sessions (reranker validation, recall sweeps, 3 autoresearch sessions,
fused-pool rerank, backbone shootout). \textbf{Anthropic API $\approx$\$235} (Haiku reranks during early
blocks + dev generation + judge + extractor); \textbf{GPU $\approx$\$60}. \textbf{Ops discipline:} a local
``reaper'' continuously pulls results and auto-destroys each box on completion or a hard deadline --- several
flaky/dead hosts were destroyed pre-billing; \textbf{no runaway cost}. \textbf{Battles fought:} CUDA
driver$\leftrightarrow$torch mismatch, Qwen-72B KV-cache OOM (mem-tuned), InternVL3 image-token overflow,
2 deadline-cut sessions (over-scope), 4 MagicLens dependency-snapshot failures. \textbf{Net:} the project moved
from a 0.044/0.872 baseline to a \textbf{0.20+ \dev\ / 0.92 \off} pipeline, with a working free-marginal-cost
reranker and a new metadata lever; remaining upside is in different \emph{levers} (metadata-aware rerank,
MagicLens), not more tuning of the converged visual design.
